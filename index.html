About City Beat Independent Living

A Compliance-Focused Pathway Toward Long-Term Independence

City Beat Independent Living, LLC is a Michigan-based reentry housing organization providing structured transitional housing and supportive services for returning citizens.

Our compliance-focused program is designed to promote stability, reduce recidivism, and support successful community reintegration. The 4-Phase Pathway to Independence

A proven, milestone-driven program guiding returning citizens from day one through long-term autonomous community living.

PHASE 1

Intake & Stabilization

Days 1 – 30

Immediate safe lodging, mental health assessment, MDOC agent registration, obtaining state ID/SSN, and initial curfew orientation.

  • Comprehensive Intake Assessment
  • MDOC Agent Coordination Protocol
  • Sober Living Commitment Signed
PHASE 2

Workforce & Life Skills

Resume building, job search, interview practice, conflict resolution classes, and life skills workshops.

  • Computer Lab Literacy Training

Employment & Savings

Days 91 – 180

Autonomous Transition

Graduation into permanent independent housing, alumni mentorship involvement, and continuous aftercare support.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers for parole officers, case workers, family members, and prospective residents.

We serve adult returning citizens released from Michigan correctional facilities, individuals participating in alternative diversion or transitional sentencing programs, and individuals seeking structured sober living environments. Candidates must demonstrate willingness to adhere to community rules, participate in case management, and remain substance-free.

We maintain full alignment with Michigan Department of Corrections (MDOC) standards. We provide assigned parole and probation agents with regular compliance logs, curfew sign-in sheets, toxicology screening results, and prompt notifications regarding employment milestones or rule infractions.

City Beat enforces a zero-tolerance substance policy (NAICS 623220 use case). All residents agree to random toxicology and alcohol testing. Residents have immediate access to certified recovery peer support groups and 12-step resources to protect their sobriety and safety.

Phase 1 residents observe a standard evening curfew (typically 9:00 PM unless working an approved verifiable evening shift). Curfew privileges gradually expand as residents advance through Phases 2 and 3 and show sustained accountability. All visits occur in designated public visitation areas during posted visiting hours.

While the standard transitional housing timeline spans 6 to 12 months, individual lengths of stay are tailored according to the resident’s case management plan, employment status, housing voucher availability, and MDOC directives. Our goal is smooth graduation into permanent independent living.
