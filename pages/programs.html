Living Options & Continuum of Care

Reentry Housing & Supportive Programs

Explore our structured transitional housing and wrap-around support services designed in alignment with Michigan Department of Corrections (MDOC) supervision requirements.

Program Pillar 1

Transitional Housing Operations

A safe, predictable, and supportive residential haven for returning citizens navigating their critical transition window.

Our residences at 3367 Kendall in Detroit provide clean, fully furnished accommodations. Each resident is provided with a secure personal room, high-speed internet for job seeking and telehealth, access to common culinary facilities, and a monitored, structured environment.

  • Furnished Living: High-comfort beds, dressers, linens, and study desks.
  • MDOC Aligned: Regular sign-in sheets, verifiable curfew tracking, and agent verification.
  • Safety & Security: Monitored entryways, fire safety, and on-site house managers.

Program Pillar 2

Sober Living & Accountability

Certified under NAICS 623220 (Residential Mental Health and Substance Abuse Facilities - Sober Living related use case).

Sobriety is the cornerstone of successful community reintegration. Our zero-tolerance substance environment protects residents from triggers and negative social pressures. We integrate 12-Step recovery meetings, peer mentorship, and healthy lifestyle choices.

  • Screening Protocols: Random toxicology testing and alcohol breathalyzers.
  • Peer Support Groups: On-site recovery circles fostering brotherly accountability.
  • Relapse Prevention: Personalized wellness and crisis prevention roadmaps.
Program Pillar 3

Workforce Development & Job Readiness

Financial independence is the most proven deterrent to recidivism.

Our workforce development program pairs residents with professional career coaches to build competitive resumes, hone interview techniques, and earn essential workplace credentials.

  • Fair-Chance Employer Network: Direct connections to manufacturing, logistics, and skilled trades.
  • Computer Resource Lab: Digital literacy training, online applications, and video interview preparation.
  • Work Transportation: Assistance with bus passes and transit coordination for early/night shifts.
Program Pillar 4

Case Management & Audit-Ready Compliance

Transparent communication, individualized plans, and seamless agency reporting.

Each resident is assigned an experienced case manager upon admission. Together, they create an Individualized Reentry Plan (IRP) tracking goals across employment, savings, health, legal obligations, and permanent housing search.

  • Agent Progress Reports: Bi-weekly or monthly updates sent directly to MDOC parole agents.
  • Vital Records Assistance: Helping residents acquire Michigan driver's licenses, birth certificates, and Social Security cards.
  • Permanent Lease Transitions: Matching Phase 4 residents with landlords and affordable independent apartments.

Structure & Daily Life

A Day in the Life at City Beat

Predictability and routine build the self-discipline needed for sustainable independent living.

7:00 AM – 8:00 AM
Morning Routine & Breakfast: Personal hygiene, room inspection preparation, and breakfast.
8:30 AM – 9:15 AM
Community House Meeting: Daily reflection, announcements, work assignments, and inspirational goal setting.
9:30 AM – 3:30 PM
Work & Skill Hours: Employed residents report to workplace; job-seeking residents participate in the Computer Resource Lab or attend trade training.
4:00 PM – 5:30 PM
Case Management & Check-Ins: 1-on-1 counselor meetings, MDOC verification, and savings escrow deposits.
6:00 PM – 7:30 PM
Community Dinner & Life Skills Workshop: Group meal followed by classes in budgeting, credit, or nutrition.
8:00 PM – 9:00 PM
Sober Living Support / 12-Step: Evening recovery group, reading, reflection, and quiet fellowship.
9:00 PM / 10:00 PM
Curfew & Evening Sign-In: Mandatory sign-in, facility secure lockdown, and quiet hours.
