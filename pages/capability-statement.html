About Us

City Beat Independent Living, LLC is a Michigan-based reentry housing organization providing structured transitional housing and supportive services for returning citizens. We specialize in case management, workforce readiness, life skills training, and sober living support within a safe and accountable environment. Our compliance-focused program is designed to promote stability, reduce recidivism, and support successful community reintegration. We are committed to creating pathways toward independence, rehabilitation, and long-term community impact.

Core Competencies

  • • Transitional Housing Programs: Structured, safe residential care.
  • • Reentry Support Services: Vital documentation and barrier removal.
  • • Case Management & Resident Support: Personalized action plans.
  • • Workforce Development & Job Readiness: Career coaching & placement.
  • • Life Skills Training & Mentorship: Financial literacy & habits.
  • • Sober Living & Accountability Support: Zero-tolerance clean environment.
  • • Community Reintegration Services: Restoring positive community ties.
  • • Transportation & Referral Assistance: Transit to work, court & clinics.
  • • Program Documentation & Compliance Reporting: Audit-ready logs.
