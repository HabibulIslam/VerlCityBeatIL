Executive Message

A Vision Built on Second Chances & Structure

"Reentry is not simply an event that happens on the day of release. It is a long, deliberate journey of healing, unlearning negative patterns, and rebuilding a dignified life."

Under the visionary leadership of Jennifer Moore, City Beat Independent Living, LLC was founded to solve one of Michigan’s most critical social challenges: providing returning citizens with safe, substance-free, and accountable transitional housing that satisfies Michigan Department of Corrections (MDOC) supervision requirements while delivering authentic human warmth.

Operating under the administrative umbrella of House of Jennifer, Inc., we recognize that true independence requires more than just a bed. It requires comprehensive case management, financial literacy, workforce readiness, and an environment that fosters mutual respect.

"Our compliance-focused program is designed to promote stability, reduce recidivism, and support successful community reintegration. We are committed to creating pathways toward independence, rehabilitation, and long-term community impact."
Our Guiding Principles

Values That Define Every Decision

Just like our inspiration communities, our culture is rooted in dignity, moral accountability, and steadfast stewardship.

Respect & Dignity

We uphold the inherent dignity of every returning citizen, affirming their human worth and potential regardless of their past mistakes.

Accountability

Integrity and strict adherence to house rules, MDOC curfew guidelines, and substance-free commitments protect the entire community.

Compassion

We meet residents where they are, providing trauma-informed guidance, patience, and encouraging mentorship during difficult moments.

Quality & Compliance

Our facilities, case logs, and operations are audit-ready and maintained to the highest federal, state, and county standards.

Empowerment

We don't create dependency; we build self-efficacy through employment, credit restoration, and personal ownership.

Community Reinvestment

By reducing recidivism, we restore fathers, mothers, sons, and daughters back to Detroit neighborhoods as productive citizens.

House of Jennifer, Inc. & Certifications

City Beat Independent Living, LLC operates with full administrative oversight under House of Jennifer, Inc., a recognized Michigan corporation dedicated to community uplifting and residential rehabilitation.

As an organization, we hold key socioeconomic and federal business designations that enable seamless partnerships with state agencies, federal grantmakers, and corporate social responsibility foundations:

  • SAM.gov Active: Verified federal vendor eligible for federal agency contracts and grants.
  • MBE Certified: Certified Minority Business Enterprise promoting diversity and supplier equity.
  • SBA WOSB Certified: Women-Owned Small Business Federal Contracting certification.

City Beat Independent Living, LLC is a Michigan-based reentry housing organization providing structured transitional housing, workforce readiness, and sober living support.
